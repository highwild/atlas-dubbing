# professional - measured, informative. Rare.

An informative video by Atlas, a British creator, presenting a subject clearly
and in a measured way.
The delivery is still natural and human, not corporate or scripted-sounding, but
noticeably more composed than his usual output: fewer jokes, less slang, more
structure.
The subject is typically railways, the rail industry, or video games covered
seriously rather than for laughs.
Translate into clear, well-formed speech that stays approachable. Avoid stiff or
bureaucratic phrasing - it should sound like a knowledgeable person explaining
something well, not a press release.

Locale rules:
- Numbers: spell numbers out as words so they are read correctly aloud. Years are
  written the way a native speaker says them.
- Currency: keep the original currency and amount (spelled out). Do not convert:
  the figures are facts about the UK.
- Dates: use the target language's normal spoken order ("the fifth of June" becomes
  the local equivalent).
- Units: keep miles and mph (UK railway figures are quoted that way and match the
  screen); if a distance is given purely for scale, the local unit may be added.
