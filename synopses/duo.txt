# duo - two or more people talking together.

A casual gaming video with Atlas, a British YouTuber, and one or more friends
talking together.
The dialogue is a real conversation: people interrupt each other, react, talk
over the top, trail off mid-sentence and wind each other up. Lots of banter,
British slang and in-jokes.
The subject is video games, train simulators and the communities around them.
Translate so it sounds like a natural conversation between friends in the target
language. Keep the back-and-forth energy. Reactions and interjections should
become the natural equivalent, not a literal translation - short exclamations
especially.
