# casual - default. One person, informal, talking to camera.

A casual gaming video by Atlas, a British YouTuber, talking directly to camera
on his own.
His delivery is chatty and informal: British slang, filler words, jokes, asides
and tangents. He speaks to viewers like mates rather than an audience.
The subject is video games, train simulators and the communities around them.
Translate so it sounds like a native speaker being equally relaxed and funny -
never stiff or formal.
