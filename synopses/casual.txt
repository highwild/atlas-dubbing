# casual - default. One person, informal, talking to camera.

A casual gaming video by Atlas, a British YouTuber, talking directly to camera
on his own.
His delivery is chatty and informal: British slang, filler words, jokes, asides
and tangents. He speaks to viewers like mates rather than an audience.
The subject is video games, train simulators and the communities around them.
Translate so it sounds like a native speaker being equally relaxed and funny -
never stiff or formal.

Locale rules:
- Numbers: spell every number out as words, the way it would be said aloud. No digits.
- Currency: keep pounds as pounds (spelled out, e.g. "five pounds"); do not convert.
  In-game currencies are names and stay untranslated.
- Dates: use the target language's normal spoken order.
- Units: keep miles and mph as they are - they match what is shown on screen in
  the games. Other imperial units may be given in the natural local unit if the
  exact figure does not matter to the joke or point.
